// 01. About me

More than a developer. A solution architect.

I'm Daniel Maina Waweru, a full-stack software engineer and systems architect from Kenya. I start with the problem, not the tech stack: who it's for, what it must do, and how it needs to grow. Then I design the architecture and build every layer of it.

That has meant modular monoliths that get products to market fast and microservices that carry them to scale, secure payment flows on M-Pesa and Stripe, and AI that answers from real data. What I'm proudest of isn't the code. It's that these systems are in use today, solving real problems for real people.

The platforms I built at Clinifix, WeMove and Kenoobi Group are live and now in maintenance, so I'm available for a new role to architect and ship the next one.
